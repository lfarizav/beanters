Luis Felipe Ariza Vesga, from Bogotá

PhD in Electrical Engineering. Kubestronaut. KubeCon Europe 2026 speaker.

Background

He holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering, focused on telecommunications, and has worked since 2014 on next-generation cellular networks. He has private 4G and 5G cellular networks in band b8 configured and running, with a 20 W power amplifier (PA), VoLTE, SMS, IPTV and data. He took part in implementing the first LTE-based 5G Broadcast pilot in Colombia (Santiago de Tolú, 2020–2021), pilot documented by 5G-MAG, and before that he worked at EURECOM, in France, on OpenAirInterface, the open source software for 4G and 5G networks.

He is a Kubestronaut, the distinction the CNCF gives to whoever holds all five Kubernetes certifications at the same time: KCNA, KCSA, CKA, CKAD and CKS. He applies AI and cloud native technologies to telecommunications following the guidelines of the Linux Foundation’s Sylva project.

Three areas of action

Cloud-native, AI-native and telco: the work lives where the cloud, artificial intelligence and radio meet.

Cloud-native

Kubestronaut: he holds all five CNCF Kubernetes certifications at the same time. He runs cellular network functions with Helm, Flux and GitOps, the subject of his talk at KubeCon Europe 2026 in Amsterdam.

AI-native

AI applied to telecommunications: deploying cellular networks from natural-language instructions, MLOps for the radio access network, and agentic engineering training for software teams. In physical AI he designs and builds drones and robots from scratch, controlled by local AI.

Telco

Private 4G and 5G cellular networks in band b8: a 20 W power amplifier (PA), with VoLTE, SMS, IPTV and data, all configured and running. He took part in implementing the first LTE-based 5G Broadcast pilot in Colombia (Santiago de Tolú, 2020–2021), pilot documented by 5G-MAG, and he worked at EURECOM, in France, on OpenAirInterface.

Community

Since 2022 he has organized the Colombia OpenInfra User Group, Colombia’s open infrastructure community. In 2025 he organized KCD Colombia, the local gathering of the Kubernetes community, and spoke there.
