    About the author

    Luis Felipe Ariza Vesga

    Telecommunications engineer with a doctorate and author of technical books on Kubernetes, GitOps, security, private 5G networks and Linux.

    His method is the same across the series: tell every concept through an everyday story, publish it in Spanish and English at the same time, and check every behavior against the official documentation. The analogy never replaces rigor.

    Each book in the “Here’s What’s Actually Going On…” series is dedicated to a species driven extinct by human activity, which is why each of those covers carries an animal.
