FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about Beanters

Answers about who teaches, what is taught, what it costs and how to enroll, based on the information published on this site.

Who teaches the courses and programs?

Luis Felipe Ariza Vesga is the instructor for all programs. He holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering, is a Kubestronaut and spoke at KubeCon Europe 2026, and has worked since 2014 on next-generation cellular networks. Read more on About Luis.

What topics does Beanters teach?

Cloud-native, AI-native and telco: the work lives where the cloud, artificial intelligence and radio meet.

The courses and programs are:

  • Cellular networks: from 2G to 6G
  • Agentic engineering
  • Cloud-native Kubernetes
  • CKA, CKAD and CKS preparation
  • AI, security and risk in telecommunications

The full catalog is on Courses.

In which languages are the courses and the books?

The courses and programs are taught in Spanish and English. The four published books also exist in both languages.

What training formats are there?

Self-paced courses and live programs with labs, in Spanish and English.

25 self-paced modules with labs and quizzes. The first three are free. Each course opens with 30 seats.

One live day for telco and cloud-native engineers, with labs drawn from the cellular networks, Kubernetes and agentic engineering courses. This is a waitlist for the next cohort, date to be announced; it is not a confirmed event yet. From US$199 per person; team quote on request.

How much does the cellular networks course cost?

It has a free preview with 3 starter modules. Full access (25 modules, labs and quizzes) costs $89 USD. The team and university plan costs $490 USD, with 10 seats and an invoice available. The details are on the course page.

How much do the live programs cost?

These are the prices published on the Courses page:

  • Agentic engineering: $249 USD · individual, 66+ hours
  • Cloud-native Kubernetes: $199 USD · individual, 50+ hours
  • CKA, CKAD and CKS preparation: $149 USD · per cert, ~30 h per cert
  • AI & Security for Cloud-Native Telco: $279 USD · individual, 48+ hours
  • Risk Management for Telco & Cloud-Native AI: $229 USD · individual, 40+ hours
  • Live workshop: Open5GS/IMS on Kubernetes with AI agents: from $199 USD per person, 1 day · date to be announced

The CKA, CKAD and CKS preparation does not guarantee that you pass the exam.

Are there prices for teams and universities?

Live, role-based programs with hands-on labs, hands-on deployment scenarios and direct access to the instructor. Each program has a link to ask for team pricing by email. Each course opens with 30 seats. Need a private course? Message Luis on WhatsApp or by email.

How do I enroll and how do I pay?

How enrollment works: Click "Enroll" to send a pre-filled email. Luis replies within 24 hours with a payment link and access credentials. Accepts card, bank transfer, and local payment methods (Colombia: Nequi / Daviplata). Corporate invoicing available.

Which books are there and where can I buy them?

The “Here’s What’s Actually Going On…” series explains Kubernetes through everyday analogies. Four books, in Spanish and English, sold on Amazon.

  • Here’s What’s Actually Going On with Kubernetes
  • Here’s What’s Actually Going On with Kubernetes Administration
  • Here’s What’s Actually Going On with Kubernetes Application Development
  • Here’s What’s Actually Going On with Kubernetes Security

Each book is sold as a Kindle edition, paperback and hardcover. The free samples contain chapter 1 only. The published books are sold complete on Amazon; the others are not on sale yet. See the whole collection.

Where can I see the talks?

Conferences and community meetups, each with its slides or video. They are on Presentations.

How much does the security review of apps built with AI cost?

A fixed-price review, from US$249, of apps built with AI tools such as Lovable, Supabase or Claude Code output. It covers exposed keys, database access rules and deployment hardening, and is delivered as a written report. The report lists findings and recommendations; it does not guarantee that the app becomes secure.

How do I contact Luis?

Write to Luis with the topic, the size of the group and the language. He will suggest a course, a live program or a mix of both. By WhatsApp or email: +57 313 398 0338 and lfarizav@gmail.com. Beanters works from Bogotá.
