Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Beanters Telecommunications · Colombia

Note: in case of any discrepancy between the Spanish and English versions, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Who We Are

Beanters Telecommunications ("Beanters", "we", "us") is a cloud-native telco orchestration company headquartered in Bogotá, Colombia. This policy applies to the website beanters.com and any services we offer.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form:

  • Full name and organization
  • Email address
  • Use case description
  • Any message you submit

We do not use cookies, third-party analytics scripts, or advertising pixels. We do not collect data automatically beyond standard server logs (IP address, timestamp, browser type) which are retained for up to 30 days.

Anonymous counters: the site keeps three aggregate counters: one for page visits, one for books opened in the reader and one for votes on books not yet published. Each counter is just one integer per key (for example, total visits, the opens of one book or the votes for one book). No cookies, IP address, user agent, or any personal data or identifier is stored. A per-tab flag, kept only in your browser's sessionStorage, avoids counting more than one visit per tab session; it disappears when the tab is closed. To avoid repeating a vote, your browser keeps one mark per voted book in its localStorage; that mark never leaves your browser and you can delete it whenever you wish.

Reader reviews: Visitors may submit a rating, a comment and an optional name. These are stored with the date only, reviewed before publication, shown publicly if approved, and removed on request via the contact listed on this page. No other data is stored.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your demo or consulting request
  • Send you information relevant to the products or services you asked about

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR / Colombian Habeas Data)

Processing is based on your explicit consent provided when you submit the contact form, and on our legitimate interest in responding to business inquiries. Under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012 (Habeas Data), you have the right to know, update, rectify, and delete your personal data at any time.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for up to 12 months after last contact. You may request deletion at any time.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate information
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data
  • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to any processing at any time

7. Contact

To exercise your rights or ask privacy questions, email: lfarizav@gmail.com
or write to: Beanters Telecommunications, Bogotá, Colombia.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
