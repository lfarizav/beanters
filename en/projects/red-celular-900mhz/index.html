PROJECT 1

One cellular network on the 900 MHz band, many services: radio, TV, calls and video conferences without internet

This project extends what a community cellular network can do by offering many services on it, all in the 900 MHz band. Inside the community and without internet, the same network delivers radio and TV as IPTV, local calls, local video conferences and SENA training content; when there is an internet link, it also gives full internet access. It is built with free software and general-purpose hardware with a software-defined radio (SDR).

As the author's proposal, the state controls and oversees every site from the ministry (MinTIC), and the ANE verifies spectrum use.
